## Getting started

**How do I get an account?**
Your administrator creates it with your phone number and a 5-digit PIN. Sign in from the extension's popup.

**"Locked" after wrong PINs**
After too many wrong PINs the account locks for a few minutes. Wait, then try again, or ask your administrator to check your PIN.

**What does the phone need?**
A browser (Chrome or Safari) and a microphone. No app to install.

---

## QR code and phone

**"This QR code has expired" or "This QR code was already opened on another phone"**
A QR code works once, within 10 minutes. In the extension, press **Regenerate** (or **New form**) and scan again.

**"This link has expired. Scan the QR code again."**
A phone link lasts 30 minutes. Click **Scan form on this page** again on the computer.

**The QR code opened in WhatsApp or Google Lens and the mic doesn't work**
In-app browsers can't record. Tap **Copy link** and open the link in Chrome or Safari.

**"Microphone access was blocked."**
Allow the microphone for this site in your browser's settings. The phone page shows the steps for Chrome and Safari. Then tap **Try again**.

**Recording stopped when the screen locked or a call came in**
What was recorded so far is still sent. Tap the mic again to continue.

**"Speech service is busy"**
Wait a minute, then tap the answer to retry.

## Filling the form

**A field shows "Check this"**
The answer may be wrong. Tap it on the phone to fix it, or say it again.

**A dropdown shows "Pick one"**
What you said matched more than one option, or none. Tap the right option on the phone.

**"Waiting: this field isn't on the page yet"**
The field is on a later step of the form. It fills in by itself when that step appears.

**The phone says "The computer hasn't responded"**
Make sure the computer is awake, Chrome is open, and the form tab is still open. Then tap **Send again**.

**"Tab closed"**
The form tab on the computer was closed, or the extension was signed out. Nothing more will be sent. Scan a new QR code from the computer.

**Captcha and OTP**
Type these on the computer. They can't be filled by voice.

**A field wasn't filled**
Some websites block automatic filling for certain fields. The popup shows "Not filled" with the reason. Type that field by hand.

**Something filled wrongly**
Press **Undo** in the extension to put the page back. Or use **Start over** on the phone, then **Clear phone and computer form**.

---

## Photos and documents

**"The website refused it: …"**
The site has its own rules for that field. Read the reason, then tap the field on the phone and **Replace** the file.

**"Click 'Upload' next to this field on the page"**
Some sites need you to press their own Upload button after the file is added. Click it on the computer.

**My PDF is too big**
A PDF you choose is sent as it is. Take photos of the pages instead, and the phone will make a PDF that fits.

**Photo tips**
Put the document on a dark table, in good light, and fill the frame.

## Privacy

Voice recordings are never stored. Answers are deleted within about an hour, and files as soon as the computer has them. Read the full [privacy policy]({{SITE}}/privacy).
