# Voice Form Filler

## Fill web forms by speaking on your phone

Open a form on your computer, scan the QR code, and say the answers in Tamil, English or Tanglish. The form fills itself as you speak.

[Add to Chrome]({{STORE_URL}})

---

## How it works

1. **Scan.** Open the form in Chrome, click Voice Form Filler, then click **Scan form on this page**. A QR code appears.
2. **Speak.** Scan it with your phone camera. Tap the mic and say the answers: *"My name is Ramesh Kumar, phone 98765 43210, district Salem."*
3. **Fill.** Press **Fill form** once. New answers then fill in automatically. Check the form and submit it yourself. **Undo** puts the page back the way it was.

No app to install on the phone. It works in the phone's browser.

---

## Features

### Speak the way you talk
- Tamil, English or Tanglish, even mixed in one sentence
- Say numbers in pieces; spell names letter by letter
- Correct yourself: *"no, not Jahan, Jagan"*, *"the last digit is 5"*
- Voice commands: *"start over"*, *"send it"*, *"what is left?"*

### Works on real forms
- Dependent dropdowns (State → District → Taluk → Village), filled in order
- Unclear matches come to your phone as **"Did you mean…?"** buttons and are never guessed
- Custom dropdowns, multi-step forms, and fields that appear later
- React, Vue, Angular and plain HTML forms

### Photos and documents from your phone
- Take a photo or choose a file; rotate, crop and add pages
- Converted on the phone to the site's rules (for example "JPG, max 50 KB"), or several photos combined into one PDF
- Location and camera details are removed

### Made for busy counters
- Up to 5 forms at once, one per browser tab
- **Start over** clears the answers for the next person, using the same QR code
- The phone screen can be set to தமிழ் or English

## Your data

- Voice recordings are never stored.
- Answers and their text are deleted automatically within about an hour, or about 2 hours after your customer sends them.
- Uploaded files are deleted as soon as the computer receives them.
- Nothing is sold or used for advertising.
