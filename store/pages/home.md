# Voice Form Filler

## Fill web forms by speaking on your phone

Open a form on your computer, scan the QR code, and say the answers in Tamil, English or Tanglish. Click **Fill form**, and the answers go into the form.

[Add to Chrome]({{STORE_URL}})

---

## How it works

1. **Scan.** Open the form in Chrome, click Voice Form Filler, then click **QR for this page** and **Generate QR**. A QR code appears.
2. **Speak.** Scan it with your phone camera. Tap the mic, then say each field's name and its answer: *"Name Ramesh Kumar, mobile 98765 43210, district Salem."* The recording stops by itself after 4 seconds of silence, and the answers appear on the phone.
3. **Send.** Check the answers on the phone and tap **Send to desktop**.
4. **Fill.** Click **Fill form** on the computer. The page changes only when you click it. Check the form and submit it yourself. **Undo** puts the page back the way it was.

No app to install on the phone. It works in the phone's browser.

## Features

### Speak the way you talk
- Tamil, English or Tanglish, even mixed in one sentence
- Say numbers in pieces; spell names letter by letter
- Correct yourself: *"no, not Jahan, Jagan"*, *"the last digit is 5"*
- Fix one answer by typing it; typing uses no voice minutes
- 6 voice tips show how to get answers right the first time

### Works on real forms
- Dependent dropdowns (State → District → Taluk → Village), filled in order
- Unclear matches come to your phone as **"Which one did you mean?"** buttons and are never guessed
- Custom dropdowns, multi-step forms, and fields that appear later
- React, Vue, Angular and plain HTML forms
- Fields that ask for Tamil are filled in Tamil; all others in English
- Captcha and OTP: you type them on the computer

### Photos and documents from your phone
- Take a photo or choose a file; rotate, crop and add pages
- Converted on the phone to the site's rules (for example "JPG, max 50 KB"), or several photos combined into one PDF
- Paper documents are found in the photo and straightened, with a clean **Scan look**
- A PDF that is too big is made smaller, with every page kept
- Location and camera details are removed

### Documents from your scanner
- **Fetch from scan** shows new scans from your scanner's folder, with nothing to install
- Pick the scans and the upload field; each one is cropped, straightened and sized to the field's rules
- Scans stay on your computer. They go only into the website's upload field

### Customers fill forms before their turn
- **QR for other** makes a QR code for a form you have used before, one QR per customer, with no website open and no login needed yet
- Customers in the queue speak or type on their own phones, then tap **Send to operator**
- At their turn, their name is under **Customer answers waiting**. Open the form, click **Use**, then **Fill form**

### Answers wait if a website logs you out
- Answers your customer has sent wait for about 2 hours, even if the form tab closes
- **Use** carries them on in a new tab, with no voice minutes used
- **Customer needs to correct** gives the customer's phone a new QR code or link

### Made for busy counters
- Up to 5 forms at once, one per browser tab
- **Typing only** for a noisy counter: the customer types on their own phone, with no mic and no voice minutes
- **Start over** clears the answers for the next person, using the same QR code
- **Connect phone again** if the phone page was closed; the answers are kept

### Tamil tools
- **Tamil typing:** speak Tamil into the computer's microphone and copy the text anywhere, such as a letter or a message. It uses your voice minutes
- **Tamil helper** on the phone: type an English word and see it in Tamil
- The phone screen can be set to தமிழ் or English

## Your data

- Voice recordings are never stored.
- Answers and their text are deleted automatically within about an hour, or about 2 hours after your customer sends them.
- Uploaded files are deleted as soon as the computer receives them, or with the answers.
- Scans from your scanner stay on your computer and are never sent to us.
- Nothing is sold or used for advertising.
